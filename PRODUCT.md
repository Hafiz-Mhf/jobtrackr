# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Junior-to-mid-level developers and tech job seekers actively applying to multiple roles. They are navigating a stressful life transition (job hunting) and need a streamlined, organized way to manage applications without spreadsheets or paid tools.

## Product Purpose
Eliminate the manual chore of job tracking. JobTrackr provides an automated pipeline (client-side JD parsing, Kanban board tracking, local tag-matched interview prep, and stale-application reminders) with zero external AI API costs. Success is a user feeling organized, prepared, and in control of their career search.

## Brand Personality
Calm confidence. The app feels like a well-lit, airy co-working space — refreshing, focused, and professional. Think Notion meets Linear, but warmer.

## Anti-references
Dark-mode by default, neon accents, glassmorphism blobs, card shadows everywhere, gradient hero banners, and "cute" UI messages (e.g., "Oops! Nothing here 😅").

## Design Principles
- **Calm Focus in Chaos**: Create a serene, well-lit UI that reduces cognitive load during a stressful job hunt.
- **Instant Magic**: Power all smart features client-side to deliver instant feedback without loading spinners.
- **Intentional Breathing Space**: Let generous whitespace and clean typography do the heavy lifting rather than heavy lines or decorative borders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast Integrity**: High contrast ratios (≥4.5:1) for all text and form fields, avoiding the common trend of light gray text on light backgrounds.
- **Motion Restraint**: Full support for users with vestibular disorders by disabling the background aurora drift and page transitions when `prefers-reduced-motion` is active.
