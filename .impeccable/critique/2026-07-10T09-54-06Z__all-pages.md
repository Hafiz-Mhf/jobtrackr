## Overall Impression
JobTrackr has a strong foundation with its clean color palette (plus-violet accent) and instant client-side actions. However, the UX suffers from rigid navigation flows (e.g., locking input pages, lack of search/filter) and some visual styling choices that feel generic or AI-scaffolded.

---

## What's Working
1. **Color Palette & Theme**: The light-first palette with the warm-white base (`#F8F7FF`) and indigo-violet accent (`#5B4EE8`) works extremely well to evoke calm focus.
2. **Instant Client-Side Actions**: The tag-matching prep panel and parser run instantly, skipping annoying loading states.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Card Navigation vs Drag Conflict
- **Why it matters**: The entire card is a draggable container, but it contains a link to navigate to the details page. This creates a conflict where users trying to drag a card will accidentally trigger a page navigation, or vice-versa, causing high frustration.
- **Fix**: Add a dedicated drag handle icon on the card, or make only the text click-to-navigate while the rest is draggable.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Lack of Search & Filters on All Jobs List
- **Why it matters**: As the list of job applications grows, a raw, unsorted list forces the user to scan through everything, violating the "Recognition rather than recall" heuristic.
- **Fix**: Add a simple text search bar and status filters (dropdown or tabs) to the top of the All Jobs page.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Rigid Onboarding / Add Job Flow
- **Why it matters**: Once a user selects "Fill manually" or extracts a JD, they cannot return to the JD paste textarea without refreshing the page, trapping them in the current state.
- **Fix**: Add a "Back" button to return to the paste screen from the form.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Hardcoded Status Updates in Reminders
- **Why it matters**: In the Reminders view, clicking "Update Status" automatically updates the job to "interview" status, which may not be the actual current status of the application.
- **Fix**: Replace the direct button action with a dropdown selection or open a quick dialog to choose the new status.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Artificial Styling Accents (AI Slop)
- **Why it matters**: The thick borders (`border-l-4` on columns and `border-t-4` on prep panel) feel like decorative boilerplate.
- **Fix**: Remove the side-stripe border on `Column.tsx` and the top-stripe border on `PrepPanel.tsx`, relying instead on background tint and clean card borders.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`
